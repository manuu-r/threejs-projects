# Character lamp asset

The articulated lamp geometry is adapted from **Luxo Jr. Rig**, uploaded by **aiden915** on Sketchfab and credited by the uploader to **pistiwique**. The downloaded model is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

- Downloaded source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/luxo-jr-rig-cf02aefd940f47d688db0ce1069e9c30
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Initially referenced listing: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/luxo-jr-rig-a06676ad04b34a269061e4f16cc91e80

This project rebuilds the source's Blender-only IK setup as a web-ready, wheel-free pivot hierarchy; adds a browless layered-eye face, custom materials, grounded contact pads, and export preparation; and does not include a Pixar wordmark or in-app lamp branding. Third-party names and character-design rights remain with their respective owners.
