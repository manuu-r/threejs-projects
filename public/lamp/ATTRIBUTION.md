# Character lamp asset

The articulated arm geometry is derived from **Desk Lamp Arm 01** by Kuutti Siitonen (modeling/texturing) and Yann Kervran (rigging), published by Poly Haven under CC0.

- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/desk_lamp_arm_01

The weighted base, web-ready joint hierarchy, materials, light-face, eyes, brows, and export preparation were created for this character lamp project.
